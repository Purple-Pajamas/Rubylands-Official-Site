Branches

Due to the open source nature of the Rubylands mod, there may be special branches that have been made to allow more personalization of the game. This is a list of official and curated branches.

If you want your branch to be curated, message Raven on Discord with the details about it. Right now, there's no criteria, so an unofficial branch will only be added if she approves it.

Here is a list of all official and curated branches so far.



1.20.16.05 Marigold

The main branch of the Rubylands mod. The codename is it's official identifier to seperate it from other branches, chosen because the lead dev wanted to name it after some kind of golden flower.

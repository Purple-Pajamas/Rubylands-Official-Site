Enter the Rubylands!

Marigold is a total conversion mod for 1.20.1. Inspired by the 16.05 branch of alpha Minecraft, the mod aims to port most of it's features to a (relatively) modern version of the game, allowing this lost version to be more accessable to players.



Build!

Amazing new structures with a wide variety of blocks. New wood types, ores, bricks and more will greatly expand your pallete.



Fight!

Scary new mobs with brand new weapons, and protect yourself with new armor. Use new abilities to aid you in combat.



Explore!

A strange, mysterious new world. Climb the tallest mountains to pluck the stars from the sky, or venture down into the darkest depths and explore worlds long forgotten.
